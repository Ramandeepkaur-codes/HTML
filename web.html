introduction

The Internet is a giant global network that links millions of computers, phones, and other devices together so people can share data, talk, and find information. It works like a big highway system where data moves from one place to another.

Advantages

Main Advantages of the InternetFast Communication: Talk or video call anyone around the world instantly.Endless Information: Find answers, news, and study tools on any topic.Easy Shopping and Work: Buy goods, pay bills, and work from home.

Disadvantages

Main Disadvantages of the InternetCyber Threats: Risk of hacking, identity theft, and scams. Fake Information: False news and unverified facts spread very fast. Health and Addiction: Too much screen time leads to poor health, stress, and less real-life social contact.
